## 📌 Basic Information

**Blossom Upscaler** is a lightweight, client-side web application designed to upgrade your low-resolution images seamlessly without relying on heavy external servers or complex backend APIs. 

* **Theme & Aesthetics:** Inspired by Japanese cherry blossoms (Sakura), it features a calm pink shade palette, glassmorphism UI cards, falling cherry blossom animations, and interactive mouse-click petal burst effects.
* **Core Processing:** Uses advanced browser Canvas manipulation and custom pixel-blending algorithms to upscale images, eliminate harsh pixelation, and naturally enrich shadow contrasts (deep blacks) for a cleaner, high-resolution finish.
* **Real-time Comparison:** Includes an interactive split-screen slider to instantly compare the original and enhanced versions side-by-side.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

Using **Blossom Upscaler** is simple and straightforward:

1. **Upload an Image:** Drag and drop any image into the atelier dropzone, or click to browse and select a file from your device.
2. **Upscale & Smooth:** Click the **"Upscale & Smooth Pixels"** button to double the resolution while smoothing out jagged edges and hiding pixelation.
3. **Enhance Colors:** Click **"Enhance details & colors"** to bring out vibrant tones and rich shadow depth.
4. **Compare:** Use the interactive center slider (⇔) to drag left and right, inspecting the before and after differences in real-time.
5. **Download:** Click **"Take it with you ⤓"** to instantly save your high-resolution enhanced image to your device!
